# Open Workflow Research Notes

This template borrows workflow ideas from public, open, or official sources, then adapts them for strict NeurIPS / EMNLP-style review.

## Sources Consulted

- ACLPUB formatting guidance: https://acl-org.github.io/ACLPUB/formatting.html
- ACL submission / review / citation policy report: https://www.aclweb.org/adminwiki/images/e/e7/ACL_Guidelines_for_Submission%2C_Review_and_Citation.pdf
- Manubot: https://manubot.org/
- showyourwork!: https://show-your.work/
- Quarto manuscripts: https://quarto.org/docs/manuscripts/components.html

## What We Adopt

From ACLPUB:

- metadata must be plain text and match submission forms;
- figures and tables must be placed near first discussion and remain readable;
- references should be complete and authors should not rely blindly on automated citation indices.

From ACL policy:

- anonymity and citation behavior are part of submission integrity, not optional style;
- reviewers may search related work, so missing or distorted close work is risky.

From Manubot:

- writing should be versioned, collaborative, automated, and provenance-aware.

From showyourwork!:

- reproducibility improves when the paper build is connected to scripts, figures, and a self-contained recipe.

From Quarto manuscripts:

- computational notebooks can be treated as part of the manuscript record, but this template keeps LaTeX as the default for ACL/NeurIPS submissions.

## What We Do Not Adopt Blindly

- We do not make public continuous publishing the default for double-blind submissions.
- We do not replace venue LaTeX templates with Markdown when the venue strongly expects official style files.
- We do not treat automated citation generation as trusted.
- We do not treat workflow automation as a substitute for scientific evidence.
